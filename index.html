02 / Inside a paper

What you actually get

Not a PDF of loose questions. A booklet you can print and sit, the way the real thing arrives.

Set structure

Passages carry the questions

Twenty-five sets, each with its passage and its own four, six or seven questions. Nothing is shuffled between sets, so the reading you do is the reading the questions test.

Answer key

A reason for every answer

The key is tabular, then explained: one line saying why the right option is right and, where it matters, which trap each wrong option was built to catch.

Format

Print it, sit it, mark it

Black-and-white throughout, with a rough-work page and the key at the back. Two hours, one sitting, a net score out of the full marks.

      06 / How to use it

      Three steps, one sitting

      01

      Download and print

      Every paper is built to be printed black and white. If you have no printer, sit it on screen with a blank sheet beside you for rough work.

      02

      Sit it in one block

      Two hours, no sectional break. The paper is timed to the real thing, including the sections that eat time.

      03

      Mark it honestly

      One mark for a correct answer, a quarter deducted for a wrong one. Score the net figure — that is the number that matters on the day.

      00% of the paper

      Net score = correct − (wrong × 0.25). Unattempted questions cost nothing, which is why guessing blind is a losing trade at four options.

      07 / Questions

      Before you download

      Are the papers based on the actual CLAT pattern?

      Yes. The full-length paper follows the current pattern — 120 questions, 120 marks, two hours, five sections weighted the way the Consortium weights them. The passages and questions are original; nothing is copied from the official paper.

      What is the difference between the two current-affairs sets?

      Set 01 covers the year's headline events. Set 02 deliberately avoids those and covers schemes, infrastructure, defence, judiciary, sport and static links instead. Together they are two hundred questions with no overlap.

      Do I get explanations or only answers?

      Both. A tabular key for quick marking, then a line of reasoning for every question, naming the trap in the wrong options where there is one.

      Is all of this really free?

      Yes. Every paper on this page downloads at no cost. We ask for an email address so we can tell you when a new one goes up, and you can unsubscribe in one click.

      How do the daily question and the news quiz work?

      The question of the day rotates through a bank of two hundred, chosen by the date, so it changes at midnight. The front page of five stories and ten questions is rebuilt each morning from the RSS feeds of The Hindu and The Indian Express, and older editions stay readable from the archive strip above it. Both are free and neither needs an account.

      Can I share the files with friends?

      Send them the link rather than the file. It costs them nothing and it keeps the library going.
