# Launch checklist

## Week before
- [ ] Domain bought, DNS pointed at the host, HTTPS working on `www` and root.
- [ ] Support email created and receiving mail (try sending yourself one).
- [ ] Free-download form tested; the address arrives in your inbox.
- [ ] `legal.html` filled in — trading name, address, GSTIN if registered, provider.
- [ ] Refund and privacy wording read once more by you, not copied from anyone.

## Day before
- [ ] All five PDFs downloaded from the live site and opened on a phone.
- [ ] Each free PDF opened on a phone — no broken fonts, no missing pages.
- [ ] Site opened on a phone and on a laptop; the library filter works.
- [ ] Page speed: the home page should load in under two seconds on 4G.

## Launch day
- [ ] Announce in three places: a Telegram/WhatsApp group, one Reddit thread
      (r/clat — read its rules first), and one Instagram post.
- [ ] Lead with the free set, not the price.
- [ ] Pin a message with the link and what the free set contains.
- [ ] Answer every reply for the first six hours; that is the whole marketing.

## First month
- [ ] Publish one new paper on a fixed date and say the date publicly.
- [ ] Email the list each time something new goes up (one email, one link).
- [ ] Collect one line of feedback per buyer; put the best three on the page.
- [ ] Track: visits, downloads, email sign-ups — a sheet with one row a week.
- [ ] If downloads stall in four weeks, change where you post, not the design.

## Every month after
- [ ] New edition code on each revised paper (TFR-2027/03, /04 …).
- [ ] Correct every error reported; credit the person who reported it.
- [ ] Check the gateway payout landed; reconcile against the sales sheet.
